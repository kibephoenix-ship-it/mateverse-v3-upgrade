CHESS FOUNDATIONS

Know your pieces.

♙ Pawn

Moves forward one square. From its starting rank it may move two. Captures one square diagonally.

♖ Rook

Moves any number of empty squares horizontally or vertically.

♘ Knight

Moves in an L-shape: two squares in one direction and one sideways. It can jump over pieces.

♗ Bishop

Moves any number of empty squares diagonally.

♕ Queen

Combines rook and bishop movement: straight or diagonal.

♔ King

Moves one square in any direction. Castling is a special king-and-rook move.

Quick chess principles

  • Fight for the centre with pawns and pieces.
  • Develop knights and bishops before moving the same piece repeatedly.
  • Keep your king safe; castling often helps.
  • Before moving, ask: “What is my opponent threatening?”
